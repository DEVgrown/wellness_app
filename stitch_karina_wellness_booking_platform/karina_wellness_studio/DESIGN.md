## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a serene, sunlit, boutique movement studio. It delivers a calming, warm, and elevated wellness experience that feels deeply personal, joyful, and approachable rather than intimidating or clinical. 

The aesthetic is Modern Tactile Minimalism paired with organic warmth:
- **Atmosphere:** Airy, breathable layouts with warm cream canvases, elevated clean white cards, and natural light metaphors.
- **Personality:** Uplifting, encouraging, grounded, and cheerful. It balances Karina's high-caliber European & East African movement background with genuine human warmth.
- **Accents:** Delicate line illustrations, hand-drawn flourishes (sun, heart, smiley), and conversational script accents harmoniously juxtaposed against precision sans-serif typography.
- **Audience:** Individuals seeking private and small-group Pilates, personal training, and holistic mobility coaching—demanding effortless booking, clear program discovery, and seamless payment flows.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in nautical poise and Mediterranean-meets-Savannah warmth. The colors provide balanced contrast, visual calm, and accessibility compliant with WCAG AAA standards on key text targets.

### Role Assignments
- **Primary (`#0A2A5E`):** Deep Marine Navy. Anchors primary calls-to-action, structural brand elements, hero titles, and selected state rings.
- **Secondary (`#7DA2D0`):** Soft Sky Blue. Serves as active background tints, soft indicators, secondary badges, and calm accent highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#4E7B66`):** Soft Sage Green. Applied to confirmed booking states, successful payment tags, and progress milestones.
- **Background (`#F8F4EF`):** Linen Cream. The foundation surface for all screens, evoking calm natural warmth rather than harsh digital white.
- **Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure Crisp White. Dedicated to elevated interactive cards, modals, sheets, and inputs.
- **Borders & Dividers:** Subtle stone borders (`#E5E0D8`) on natural grounds, shifting to tinted airy slate (`#D8E2EC`) inside cards and active groups.
- **Text & Hierarchy:** Midnight Charcoal (`#0F172A`) for high-legibility headings and core body text; Slate Indigo (`#5A6E85`) for secondary metadata, subtitles, and inactive icons.
- **Functional Warning / Error:** Soft Coral Red (`#D95D51`) reserved exclusively for cancellations, error alerts, and destructive confirmations.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs clean modern geometry with friendly humanist legibility.

- **Primary Display & Headings:** `Outfit` provides geometric clarity, open apertures, and an optimistic modern edge. Track headings slightly tighter at scale (`-0.02em` to `-0.01em`).
- **Section Kickers & Badges:** `Outfit` in uppercase (`eyebrow`) with generous tracking (`0.14em`) conveys quiet luxury and clear section categorization.
- **Reading & Interface Body:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` is selected for its rounded humanist terminals and exceptional legibility across mobile touch targets, session descriptions, and calendar tables.
- **Handwritten Signature Flourishes:** Script accents (utilizing system fallbacks like `Caveat` or `Dancing Script` via CSS styling) are strictly applied as decorative flourishes—such as personal greetings ("Karina, that’s me!"), milestone stickers, and margin annotations. Script is never used for instructional UI copy or functional form fields.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a fluid grid with generous breathing room to foster an unhurried, mindful training environment.

- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Mobile (`< 640px`):** Single column, `1rem` outer canvas margin, `0.75rem` card grid gap. Touch targets remain padded to at least `48px` minimum height.
  - **Tablet (`640px – 1024px`):** 8-column layout, `1.5rem` canvas margin, `1rem` gutters.
  - **Desktop (`> 1024px`):** 12-column layout centered with max width capped at `1200px` for optimal reading comfort; `2rem` canvas margins and `1.25rem` gutters.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing between disparate content blocks follows `space-xl` (`2.5rem`), while internal card components maintain cozy containment using `space-md` (`1rem`) and `space-lg` (`1.5rem`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses ambient, warm-tinted shadows combined with surface tone separation rather than harsh boundaries:

- **Canvas Ground:** `#F8F4EF` remains completely flat and shadowless.
- **Standard Card Elevation:** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) containers floating on the cream background utilize an ambient warm blur:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(10, 42, 94, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03);`
  Border: `1px solid #E5E0D8`.
- **Interactive Hover & Drag Elevation:**
  `box-shadow: 0 12px 30px -4px rgba(10, 42, 94, 0.09), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04);`
  Transform: `translateY(-2px);`
- **Modals, Drawers & Floating Booking Bars:**
  `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -8px rgba(10, 42, 94, 0.12), 0 1px 3px rgba(10, 42, 94, 0.04);`
  Border: `1px solid #D8E2EC`.

## Shapes

The shape system expresses biological, fluid movement through soft, friendly contours.

- **Cards & Modals:** Standard corners adopt `1.25rem` (20px) to `1.5rem` (24px) corner radii (`rounded-xl`), creating inviting, organic containers.
- **Pills & Selectors:** Buttons, filter chips, tags, status pills, and stepper selectors utilize fully rounded pill formats (`border-radius: 9999px`).
- **Interactive Controls:** Form inputs, payment selection blocks, and calendar date cells use `0.75rem` (12px) to `1rem` (16px) corners.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Deep Navy (`#0A2A5E`) fill, pure white text, full pill radius (`9999px`), `48px` minimum height (`54px` on mobile sticky CTA). Subtle scale transition on press (`active:scale-[0.98]`).
- **Secondary Action:** Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`) fill with `1.5px` border in `#7DA2D0` or `#0A2A5E`, navy text, pill radius.
- **Ghost / Tertiary Action:** Transparent background with Navy `#0A2A5E` or Sky Blue `#7DA2D0` text, subtle underline or icon chevron animation.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- **Inactive:** Transparent white or `#F8F4EF` fill, `1px` border in `#E5E0D8`, muted indigo text (`#5A6E85`).
- **Active / Selected:** Soft Sky Blue fill (`#EBF2FA`), navy text (`#0A2A5E`), border in `#7DA2D0`, accompanied by an active dot or micro-checkmark.

### Calendar & Time Slot Selectors
- **Date Tiles:** Rounded square or squircle cards (`14px` radius). Inactive days rendered in neutral grey; available days framed in subtle border `#D8E2EC`; active/selected day filled in Deep Navy (`#0A2A5E`) with white numerals.
- **Time Slots:** Pill cards with `44px` height showing start time and remaining spots (e.g., "09:00 AM • 2 left").

### Payment Method Tiles (M-Pesa, Card, Apple Pay)
- Selectable cards styled with `16px` border radius, pure white background, and a discrete radio circle indicator. Selected card is outlined with a `2px` border in `#0A2A5E` and a gentle `#F2F6FB` interior wash.

### Form Inputs & Fields
- Pure white background, `1px` border in `#E5E0D8`, `12px` border radius, `48px` height.
- Focus state: `1.5px` border in `#7DA2D0` with an outer focus halo `rgba(125, 162, 208, 0.25)`.
- Labels positioned above inputs in `label-sm` with `#5A6E85`.

### Decorative Bio & Accent Motifs
- Hand-drawn outline motifs (sun, smiley face, heart, movement figure) in fine `1.5px` strokes using Deep Navy (`#0A2A5E`) or Sky Blue (`#7DA2D0`). These appear as lightweight floating badge ornaments around coach avatars, testimonial quotes, and milestone celebration toasts.
